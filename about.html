About PetPulse

Bridging the gap between abandoned animals and loving homes.

Our Background

PetPulse was founded out of a pressing need to organize and streamline animal rescue operations. Shelters often struggle with paperwork and ensuring pets are matched to the right families. We built a system that prioritizes animal well-being over everything else.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide a safe haven for abandoned animals and utilize data-driven matchmaking to pair them with responsible households.

Vision: A world where every shelter pet finds a permanent home suited to their unique behavioral needs.
