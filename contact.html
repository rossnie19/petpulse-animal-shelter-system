Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No appointment is necessary to walk through our shelter and meet the animals. However, if you are completing an adoption interview, we recommend scheduling ahead!

How much is the adoption fee?

Fees vary depending on the animal's age and medical history, but they generally cover full vaccinations, microchipping, and spay/neuter surgeries.

Can I drop off a stray animal here?

Yes, but please call us beforehand so our Intake Management team can prepare space and medical supplies for the new arrival.
